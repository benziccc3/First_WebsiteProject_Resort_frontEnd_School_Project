About us

Terraza de Sukol Resort, established in 2023, offers guests a place to relax and enjoy
the stunning panoramic view of Taal Lake and Volcano. What was once a bukuhan has become
a resort where visitors can take a break from their daily routines and appreciate the
beauty of nature.

With its swimming pools and scenic surroundings, Terraza de Sukol Resort
provides a place to spend quality time with family and friends. Whether you're looking for a
relaxing day or a place to create memorable moments, the resort welcomes you to enjoy a refreshing
experience with a beautiful view.

FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a parking area? How many vehicles can it accommodate?

Yes, we have a spacious parking area that can fit up to 10 to 15 cars and motorcycles. Parking is free for all guests.

Can we bring our own food and cook? Is there a corkage fee?

Yes, you can bring your own food and drinks. We have a grilling area and kitchen utensils available. No corkage fee.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, we are pet-friendly as long as pets are on a leash and wearing diapers to keep the pool area clean.

Is the pool open for both kids and adults? How deep is it?

Yes, we have separate pool areas. We have a kiddie pool that is 3 feet deep and an adult infinity pool that is 5 feet deep. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.

What time is check-in and check-out for overnight stay?

Check-in is at 2 PM and check-out is at 12 PM the next day. For day tour, it is from 8 AM to 5 PM.
